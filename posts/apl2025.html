APL 2025 - AOG fails agains

#aov #apl | Post day : July 15th 2025

APL 2025 : The AOG region's journey and current standings

Arena of Valor Premier League (APL) 2025 is the international summer tournament of Arena of Valor, held in Thailand with the participation of 16 teams. Among them, 12 teams received direct invitations based on their rankings in their respective domestic leagues, while the remaining 4 earned their spots through the Wildcard round, which was reserved for teams that finished 5th and 6th in their national competitions.

Teams from Indonesia were eliminated early due to the intense competition in the Wildcard stage. As a result, there are no significantly weaker teams in this APL tournament - the playing field is extremely competitive.

From the AOG region, four teams secured direct invites : Saigon Phantom (SGP), FPT x Flash (FPT), BOX Gaming (BOX), and One Star Esport (1S). Meanwhile, Super Nova (SPN) made a strong showing in the Wildcard round and joined the rest of the AOG representatives in advancing to the main stage.

I don't have an image listing the participating teams, so here's a quick breakdown of the teams from other regions competing in APL 2025. From the RPL region: Bacon Time (BAC), EArena (EA), Talon Esport (TLN), Buriram United Esport (BRU), and Full Sense (FS) will represent Thailand. From the GCS region (Taiwan): Banmei Gaming (BMG), Flash Wolf (FW), ANK Gaming (ANK), Deep Cross Gaming (DCG), Hongkong Attitude (HKA), and Cathay One Team (ONE) have qualified for the main event.

Competitions

Swiss stage

Starting from the 2024 season, APL adopted the Swiss Stage format for the qualifiers - one of the most intense and unforgiving tournament systems. In this format, teams are randomly matched in the 0-0 pool (0 wins, 0 losses). After each round, teams are grouped into pools based on their match records, such as 1-0, 0-1, 2-0, etc. Teams in the same pool will continue to face each other in subsequent rounds. A team will be eliminated from the tournament after 3 losses and be qualified after 3 wins. Only those who secure enough wins before reaching 3 losses will advance.

On the first day of competition, FPT showcased their dominance by crushing HKA with a clean 2-0 victory, while SGP had a tougher battle, edging out ONE with a 2-1 scoreline. The remaining three AOG representatives - BOX, SPN, and 1S - all suffered defeats and dropped to the 0-1 bracket.

Intra-regional clashes broke out within both RPL and GCS, and naturally, no region emerged completely unscathed after the first round.

In the second round, all five representatives from AOG suffered defeats.

In contrast to the quick defeats suffered by the other AOG representatives, FPT, despite losing 1-2 to FW, played an incredibly close and intense series across all three games. With such a strong performance against the reigning GCS champion, AOG fans have gained renewed hope in FPT's potential this season.

By the final stage of the Swiss round, 1S, BOX, and SPN had all been eliminated from the tournament (unfortunately, I don't have all the bracket images available here for you to follow the results). The two AOG teams that advanced - SGP and FPT - made it through in completely different ways.

After their loss to FW, FPT bounced back in dominant fashion, crushing GCS's second seed BMG with a clean 2-0 sweep, and then defeating RPL champion EA 3-1. With these impressive victories, FPT became the first AOG team to advance past the Swiss Stage.

Meanwhile, SGP had to endure a much tougher journey to reach the quarterfinals. They faced off against BMG and secured a convincing 3-1 victory to silence doubts about their form, before sweeping their fellow AOG representative 1S with a dominant 3-0 win.

Quarter final

In the quarterfinals, FPT faced off against ANK - a GCS representative who rose from the Wildcard stage but has been showing absolutely dominant form. FPT played on equal footing with ANK, but unfortunately, their top laner HuyHoang made a series of critical mistakes that led to the team's defeat.
