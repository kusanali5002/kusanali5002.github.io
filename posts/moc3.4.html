Quick Guide MoC 3.4 Lupine Moon - Devourer

#hsr #moc | Post day : July 25th 2025

This is a new HSR guide on Dun's blog, and his friend Atami will be in charge of the content for this section. However, what he's shared with Dun and me (Nahida) so far is still quite limited - perhaps he'll update it in the near future.

Overview about this MoC

Atami may have more insights to share later, but according to Dun, this MoC season isn't too difficult to deal with - even though he hasn't cleared it himself yet. If your characters are equipped with decent relic sets (not using mixed sets and meeting basic stat requirements), then clearing this MoC shouldn't be too hard. Let's also take a look at this season's buffs before diving into more details:

  • After the ally character in position 1 uses their Ultimate, both they and their memosprite will gain "Memory Imprint," lasting for 1 turn(s). Increases DMG dealt by targets with "Memory Imprint" by 40%, and adds 2 Hits Per Action to "Memory Turbulence," up to 10 Hit(s). At the start of each Cycle, "Memory Turbulence" deals 1 instance of True DMG to random enemy target with each Hit Per Action.

Analyze

Node 1

In node 1, we'll once again face the familiar 2 - wave enemy setup. In the first wave, Moonlit Pegasus and Furiae Praetor will appear together. Pegasus doesn't have the ability to summon allies, while Praetor can summon up to 2. Dun suggests focusing on Moonlit Pegasus first, but that recommendation may not be entirely accurate.

The second wave brings back a familiar foe - Swarm: True Sting, accompanied by another Moonlit Pegasus. This time, Dun recommends taking down True Sting first. As always, True Sting can summon smaller bugs and has 2 phases with different summoning mechanics in each.

With this lineup of enemies, Atami (and let's be clear - Atami not Dun) recommends using an Archer team for Node 1. Archer - based teams typically require characters who can generate Skill Points, such as Sparkle, Tingyun or a high - speed Pela. Remembrance Trailblazer is also considered a key support slot for this team.

You'll need to build your team around strong Skill Point generation and reserve one slot for either a sustain. However, if you're confident in your damage output and calculations, dropping the suststain option for an additional damage amplifier isn't a bad idea at all.

Other teams built around characters like The Herta, Acheron, Castorice, or even Jingliu can also perform well in Node 1. However, Superbreak - focused teams are not recommended for this stage.

Node 2

In node 2, we're still dealing with the familiar two - wave mechanic, and the enemies aren't anything too surprising either. In wave 1, you'll face creations of the Black Tide, including the Corroded Daemon, Darksun Gryphon, and Corroded Axe. Their normal attacks don't deal significant damage, and some of them even allow you to heal when attacking them. However, be cautious of their stronger abilities. All in all, bringing a sustain unit is something worth considering.

In wave 2, we face Hoolay once again. Since we've encountered this enemy multiple times before, there's not much new to say about its mechanics.

Given the nature of the enemies in Node 2, Atami recommends using a Phainon team without a sustain unit. If Phainon can enter his domain quickly enough, he'll be able to absorb all the damage that would otherwise be dealt to his allies. Running three damage - amplifying supports will further boost Phainon's DMG output, allowing him to take down enemies with ease.

Teams with high - frequency attacks such as Feixiao setups, or counter - based teams like Clara or Yunli, also perform very well here as they can effectively counter Hoolay in wave 2.
